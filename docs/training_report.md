## What this run does and does not demonstrate

**Demonstrates:** the complete pipeline runs end to end on real Azerbaijani
text; weights are randomly initialised and update; checkpoints save, load and
resume; the tokenizer is lossless over the Azerbaijani alphabet; generation and
export work.

**Does not demonstrate:** Azerbaijani fluency. The model in this run is a
pipeline-validation artifact — a few million parameters trained briefly on a
small sample. Its outputs are locally word-like but grammatically wrong, which
is the expected result at this scale. No claim of fluency is made anywhere in
this repository.

## Limitations reported by the quality pipeline

- Sources excluded pending licence review: azwikidata, mawdoo, hf_oscar_az, common_crawl_az, mc4_az. The corpus is therefore narrower than the full Azerbaijani web.
